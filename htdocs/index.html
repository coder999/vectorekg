Vectorcardiography

vectorEKG

A 12-lead ECG flattens the heart into twelve separate tracings. Transformed to X, Y and Z, the same signal is one vector sweeping a loop through the chest — drawn here beat by beat.

Drag to rotate. Double-click to pause or resume.

250 S/s · 10 s window
Recordings from PhysioNet: PTB Diagnostic ECG Database and PTB-XL.
